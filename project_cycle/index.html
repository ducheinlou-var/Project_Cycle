FOLICULAR

phase

8 days

HORMONE STATUS

Estrogen rises steadily as follicles mature. Mood, focus and motivation usually lift.

OVULATION

phase

3 days

HORMONE STATUS

Estrogen peaks and a surge of LH triggers the release of an egg. Testosterone rises slightly, boosting confidence and libido.

Estrogen

Progesterone

ENERGY NEEDED

At its peak. Ideal for intense workouts (HIIT, running), social plans and important conversations.

Fiber and antioxidants: raw vegetables, berries, leafy greens, and cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower).

Keep hydrating, since your body works hard.

LUTEAL

phase

12 days

HORMONE STATUS

Progesterone takes over, then both hormones drop before your period. PMS symptoms like bloating, irritability and cravings may appear.

Estrogen

Progesterone

helps develop female sexual characteristics, regulates
the menstrual cycle, and builds up the lining of the uterus.

prepares and maintains the lining of the uterus for a
possible pregnancy. It also slightly raises body
temperature after ovulation.

ENERGY NEEDED

Gradually decreasing. Switch to moderate activity such as pilates, strength training at a lighter pace, or walks. Protect your rest and sleep.

FOOD

Your appetite may naturally increase, so honor it with complex carbs (sweet potato, oats, brown rice).

Magnesium and B6 (nuts, seeds, banana, dark chocolate) help with mood and cramps.
